## English

**Name:** Sıralama Defteri

**Subtitle:** Rank it, play it, compare it

**Promotional text:**
A new ranking game every day. Rank blind by taste, sort into tiers, find the right order; build your own lists and compare with friends.

**Keywords:**
tier list,ranking,rank,list,blind ranking,game,daily,favorites,food,compare,tiers,trivia,numbers

**Description:**

Whatever you love, rank it.

Sıralama Defteri is both a notebook for your own rankings and a new ranking game every day.

YOUR OWN NOTEBOOK
• From coffee to döner, fruit to cities you've visited: make a list of anything.
• Rank what you've tried with photos and drag to reorder; the new champion takes the crown.
• What you didn't like sits below the "Never again" line; what you haven't tried waits in its own tab.
• Split any list into S to D tiers and categories.

A NEW GAME EVERY DAY
• Today's Numbers: ten numbers arrive one by one; place each without knowing what comes next.
• Today's True Order: planets, countries, mountains… find the right order.
• Today's Arithmetic: four operations, sorted by result.
• Everyone plays the same game; each entry in exactly the right spot scores 10.

PLAY BY TASTE
• Blind Ranking: entries arrive one by one, and once placed they stay put.
• Tier and Blind Tiers: sort dozens of lists, from soups to cities, into tiers.
• Play with the camera: entries appear above your head while you record a video with sound. The video stays on your device.

WITH FRIENDS
• Send your list or game result as a link and let them rank it too.
• See together how far your tastes match.

Games need no sign-in. To build your own notebook, sign in with Apple or Google. You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.
